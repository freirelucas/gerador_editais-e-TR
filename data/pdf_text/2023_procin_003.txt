        AWARD OF RESEARCH SCHOLARSHIP - TERM OF REFERENCE


1. PROJECT TITLE

“Studies on the improvement of the workforce and management processes at the
Federal Civil Service”.

2. PURPOSE OF THE RESEARCH

Collaborate with the Ipea and MGI teams in the preparation of studies to support the
construction of the model for filling vacancies in the 2024 National Unified Public
Contest.

3. ACTIVITIES TO BE DEVELOPED

   Perform an exploratory study on standard methods of filling vacancies, using quotas
    and ranking submission.
   Develop algorithms relative to the identified standard methods.
   Propose an alternative method for filling vacancies, based on the SISU model.
   Develop dynamic algorithm relative to the proposed alternative method.
   Produce a study on the structuring of quotas and occupation of positions, comparing
    the standard methods and the alternative method.
   Monitor the implementation of the proposed alternative model.
   Produce a study on the implementation activities of the proposed alternative model.


4. EXPECTED RESULTS

Development of a dynamic algorithm to fill vacancies in public contests.
